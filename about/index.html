About

CRHS CS Club C222 Mrs. Beardsley's Room

The Cedar Ridge High School Computer Science club is a club focused on bringing the parts of Computer Science they don't teach you in school to the broader community. We focus on practical CS applications and skills, things like git, linux, or vim, which are incredibly useful, but are not addrressed by the core curriculumn. We also aim to showcase the skills of the communnity, sponsoring events like hackathons and game jams. We also let people showcase their projects to the club and on this website. We meet bimonthly in C222 After School at 4:30.
