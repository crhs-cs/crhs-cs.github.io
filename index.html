About

We're Cedar Ridge High School's computer science club, run by students. The club came back this year after a year off. Meetings are a mix of short workshops and demo nights, where members show the club something they've built.

Join

Show up to any meeting. You don't need any experience, and you don't need a project to come watch. If you want something to start on, the resources page has free places to learn.
